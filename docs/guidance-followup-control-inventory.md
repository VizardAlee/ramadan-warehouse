## Protected and server-only boundary

Mapped available actions provide keyboard-openable Action requirements after rejection. Finite exact public bank, service/invoice receipt, account picker/mapping, journal, budget, tax and user-access failures give safe recovery. Unknown/dynamic failures retain sanitized diagnostics and the responsible authorized team’s original-evidence handoff; the browser does not inspect hidden records to manufacture a reason. Small icon controls use local guard/field explanations rather than repeated disclosures.

Serialized POS runs online with exact unique units (at most50 per order), reservation at final confirmation and retained original-unit collection/return evidence. No batch POS or unsupported detailed serial/lot reservation/exception interface is introduced. Source coverage does not establish client acceptance, other-role rendered UI or production posting correctness.
